2026–27 playoff picture

The field has 12 teams: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions; the highest-ranked team from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt; and seven at-large teams. Notre Dame receives an automatic bid if it finishes in the final Top 12, reducing at-large bids to six. Seeds 1–4 get byes; seeds 5–8 host first-round games. Rankings are not the same as seeds.
